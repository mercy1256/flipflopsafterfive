## Day 1: Mont St. Michel
- **Drive or train to Mont St. Michel**
  Start your journey from Germany and head west into Normandy. If you’re traveling by car, the drive is scenic and lets you stop in charming French countryside towns along the way.
- **Explore the island**
  Discover the medieval abbey, narrow cobblestone streets, and panoramic views from the ramparts.
- **Dinner with a view**
  Enjoy fresh seafood with a view of the tidal bay as the island takes on its evening glow.
  ![Mont St. Michel](/images/france/articles/mont-st-michel.jpg)

---

## Day 2: Etretat with a Car from Germany
- **Scenic drive to Etretat**
  Continue along the Normandy coast to Etretat, known for its dramatic chalk cliffs and seaside charm.
- **Cliff walk and viewpoints**
  Hike the coastal paths above the famous natural arches and enjoy sweeping ocean views.
- **Relax in Etretat village**
  Spend the afternoon strolling the pebble beach, browsing local shops, and savoring a seaside lunch.
- **Return journey**
  Head back toward Germany, reflecting on a memorable coastal weekend across two iconic French landmarks.
  ![Etretat Cliffs](/images/france/articles/etretat-cliffs.jpg)
  ![Etretat Village](/images/france/articles/etretat-village.jpg)

---

**Tips**
- Book parking or train tickets ahead of time for ease.
- Check the Mont St. Michel tide schedule before visiting.
- Bring layers—Normandy weather can change quickly.
