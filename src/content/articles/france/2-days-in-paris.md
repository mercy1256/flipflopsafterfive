## Day 1: Louvre Museum and Eiffel Tower at Night
- **Louvre Museum**
  Start your Paris weekend with a morning visit to the Louvre. Arrive early to see the Mona Lisa, wander through the grand galleries, and soak in centuries of art history.
- **Lunch in the 1st arrondissement**
  Enjoy a classic French lunch at a nearby café—try a Croque Monsieur or a fresh salad with a glass of wine.
- **Seine River stroll**
  Walk along the Seine toward Île de la Cité, passing bookstalls and charming bridges.
- **Eiffel Tower at night**
  End the day with an evening visit to the Eiffel Tower. Watch it sparkle after sunset and take in the glittering Paris skyline.

## Day 2: Palace of Versailles Day Trip
- **Morning train to Versailles**
  Catch the RER C train from central Paris to the Palace of Versailles. Aim to arrive early to beat the crowds.
- **Explore the palace**
  Tour the opulent Hall of Mirrors, the royal apartments, and the beautifully decorated state rooms.
- **Gardens of Versailles**
  Spend the afternoon wandering the formal gardens, fountains, and hidden groves. Rent a bike or a golf cart for a relaxed way to cover more ground.
- **Return to Paris**
  Head back to the city in the evening and enjoy a relaxed dinner in a classic Parisian bistro.
  ![Palace of Versailles](/images/france/articles/palace-of-versailles.jpg)
  ![Versailles Gardens](/images/france/articles/versailles-gardens.jpg)

---

**Tips**
- Buy Louvre and Versailles tickets in advance to avoid lines.
- Use the metro and RER for quick city travel.
- Dress comfortably for walking; Paris is best explored on foot.
