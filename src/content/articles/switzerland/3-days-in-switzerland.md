## Day 1: Lauterbrunnen and Interlaken
- **Lauterbrunnen valley**
  Begin in Lauterbrunnen with its dramatic cliffs, waterfalls, and charm. Hike to Staubbach Falls and enjoy the alpine scenery.
- **Interlaken afternoon**
  Travel to Interlaken for a lakeside walk and stunning views of the surrounding mountains.
  ![Lauterbrunnen](/images/switzerland/articles/lauterbrunnen.jpg)
  ![Interlaken](/images/switzerland/articles/interlaken.jpg)

---

## Day 2: Grindelwald and Bachalpsee
- **Morning in Grindelwald**
  Explore Grindelwald village and take the gondola to higher mountain viewpoints.
- **Hike to Bachalpsee**
  Follow the scenic trail to Bachalpsee for a crystal-clear mountain lake view, framed by snow-capped peaks.
  ![Grindelwald](/images/switzerland/articles/grindelwald.jpg)
  ![Bachalpsee](/images/switzerland/articles/bachalpsee.jpg)

---

## Day 3: Oeschinensee Hike
- **Oeschinensee adventure**
  Visit Oeschinensee and hike around the lake, taking in the pristine water and dramatic cliffs.
- **Optional boat ride**
  Rent a rowboat for a peaceful experience on the lake before heading back to Interlaken.
  ![Oeschinensee](/images/switzerland/articles/oeschinensee.jpg)

---

**Tips**
- Dress in layers for changing mountain weather.
- Use the Swiss Travel Pass for easy public transport.
- Book gondola rides and lake access ahead in summer.
