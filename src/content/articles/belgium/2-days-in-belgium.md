## Day 1: Ghent and Bruges
- **Morning in Ghent**
  Start your trip in Ghent, wandering through the medieval city center and admiring the Saint Bavo’s Cathedral and the Graslei waterfront.
- **Canal-side lunch**
  Enjoy a relaxed lunch by the river and sample classic Belgian fries with mayonnaise.
- **Afternoon in Bruges**
  Travel to Bruges and explore the fairy-tale streets, the Markt square, and the Belfry tower.
- **Evening canal cruise**
  Finish the day with a peaceful boat ride through Bruges’ illuminated canals.

  ![Bruges](/images/belgium/articles/bruges.jpg)

## Day 2: Antwerp and Brussels
- **Morning in Antwerp**
  Visit the Cathedral of Our Lady and the historic diamond district, then wander through the trendy Old Town.
- **Lunch at a café**
  Try Belgian specialties like mussels and waffles in Antwerp’s bustling squares.
- **Afternoon in Brussels**
  Head to Brussels to see the Grand Place, Manneken Pis, and the Royal Palace.
- **Evening chocolate tasting**
  End the day tasting Belgian chocolates and strolling the illuminated city center.
  ![Antwerp](/images/belgium/articles/antwerp.jpg)
  ![Brussels](/images/belgium/articles/brussels.jpg)

---

**Tips**
- Buy a train pass or point-to-point tickets for quick travel between cities.
- Try local beers alongside your meals.
- Pack comfortable shoes for cobblestone streets.
