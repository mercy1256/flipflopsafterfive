## Day 1: Amsterdam, Rijksmuseum and Zaanse Schans
- **Morning in Amsterdam**
  Explore the canals, historic bridges, and bustling neighborhoods of Amsterdam. Visit the Rijksmuseum to see masterpieces by Rembrandt and Vermeer.
- **Afternoon at Zaanse Schans**
  Take a short trip to Zaanse Schans to see traditional windmills, wooden houses, and artisanal workshops.
- **Evening canal-side dinner**
  Return to Amsterdam for dinner along the water and enjoy the city’s relaxed nightlife.

## Day 2: Keukenhof Gardens
- **Tulip fields at Keukenhof**
  Spend the day among endless tulip displays and landscaped gardens. March through May is the best season to experience the full bloom.
- **Picnic among flowers**
  Enjoy a relaxing lunch with views of the colorful flower beds.
  ![Keukenhof Gardens](/images/netherlands/articles/keukenhof-gardens.jpg)

---

## Day 3: Giethoorn
- **Travel to Giethoorn**
  Head to the storybook village of Giethoorn, famous for its canals, thatched-roof cottages, and peaceful atmosphere.
- **Boat ride and walking tour**
  Explore the village by small boat and wander quiet streets lined with flower-filled gardens.
  ![Giethoorn](/images/netherlands/articles/giethoorn.jpg)

---

**Tips**
- Buy train tickets early for the best prices.
- Rent a bike in Amsterdam for a true local experience.
- Visit Keukenhof during the tulip season for peak color.
