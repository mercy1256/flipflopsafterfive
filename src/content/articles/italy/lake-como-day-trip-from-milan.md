Lake Como is an easy day trip from Milan, and it's the one part of my Italy trip where I'd give quite different advice to what I actually did.

So this is the honest version rather than the postcard one.

---

## What I did, and why I'd change it

The standard Lake Como day is: train to Como, boat up the lake, stop at Bellagio, stop at Varenna, come back. That's roughly what I did.

**The boat took about an hour and a half in total.**

And here's my honest reaction: **the towns all looked much the same.** Bellagio is billed as the "Pearl of Lake Como", Varenna gets described as the quieter, prettier alternative, and in practice they're variations on the same thing — attractive lakeside towns with coloured buildings, narrow streets and a lot of other day-trippers doing exactly the same circuit.

None of it was bad. But I spent a good chunk of the day on boats travelling between places that turned out to be broadly interchangeable, and I don't think I got much for it.

![Como](/images/italy/articles/como.jpg)

---

## What I'd do instead: pick one town and go up

Here's the plan I'd actually recommend.

**1. Pick one town.** Any of them. Genuinely — the differences between them matter far less than the guidebooks suggest, and choosing one immediately buys you back two or three hours of boat time.

**2. Get to the highest point you can.** This is the part that's actually worth the effort. The view *of* the lake from above is the thing that makes Lake Como special — the water, the mountains dropping into it, the towns as small clusters of colour around the shore. From lake level, you're just in a nice town. From above, you understand what everyone's talking about.

Most of the towns have some way up — a funicular, a path, a road to a viewpoint. Find it and use it.

**3. Then have a slow day at the water.** Find a good restaurant with a view, sit down, and stay there. That's what the lake is for, and it's what I'd optimise the day around if I went again.

I didn't do the funicular or the walk up myself, and that's precisely the omission I'm flagging — I did the horizontal version of this day and would recommend the vertical one.

---

## Getting there from Milan

**Trains from Milan to Como take under an hour** and run frequently, which is what makes this a comfortable day trip rather than an expedition. Varenna is also directly reachable by train if you'd rather start further up the lake.

**Boats connect the towns**, and this is where the time goes. A single hop is manageable; stringing three or four together is what eats the day.

---

## Is Lake Como worth a day of your trip?

If you're in Milan for more than a couple of days, yes — it's beautiful, it's easy, and it's a complete change of pace from the city.

But temper the expectations. Lake Como is a place to sit still and look at water and mountains. Treated as a sightseeing circuit with a checklist of towns, it will underwhelm you, which is more or less what happened to me. Treated as a slow day with one good view and one good meal, it's excellent.
