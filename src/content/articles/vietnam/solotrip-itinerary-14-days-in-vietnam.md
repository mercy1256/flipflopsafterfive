## Day 1: Ho Chi Minh City
- **Explore District 1**
  Start your Vietnam solo adventure in the energetic heart of Saigon. Walk past colonial landmarks like the Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica and the Central Post Office, then sip strong Vietnamese coffee at a sidewalk café.
- **Ben Thanh Market**
  Browse local handicrafts, silk, and fresh produce. This is a great introduction to Vietnamese street food—try a bánh mì or fresh spring rolls.
  ![Ho Chi Minh City](/images/vietnam/articles/ho-chi-minh-city.jpg)

---

## Day 2: Mekong Delta and Cu Chi Tunnels
- **Mekong Delta day trip**
  Head south into the lush Mekong Delta, ride a sampan through narrow canals, and visit floating markets where fruit vendors paddle their boats.
- **Cu Chi Tunnels**
  In the afternoon, explore the famous Cu Chi Tunnel network and learn how the tunnels shaped the conflict-era landscape. Crawling through the narrow passages is an unforgettable part of the experience.
  ![Mekong Delta](/images/vietnam/articles/mekong-delta.jpg)
  ![Cu Chi Tunnels](/images/vietnam/articles/cu-chi-tunnel.jpg)

---

## Day 3: Travel to Da Nang
- **Fly to Da Nang**
  Take a quick flight from Ho Chi Minh City to Da Nang and settle in near the coast.
- **My Khe Beach**
  Relax on powder-soft sand and enjoy a sunset walk along the shoreline.
  ![Da Nang](/images/vietnam/articles/da-nang.jpg)

---

## Day 4: Golden Bridge and Ba Na Hills
- **Golden Bridge**
  Visit the iconic Golden Bridge held by two enormous stone hands. The view over the misty hills is one of Vietnam’s must-see photo moments.
- **Ba Na Hills**
  Explore the French-inspired village, ride the cable car, and wander the lantern-lined streets.
  ![Golden Bridge](/images/vietnam/articles/golden-bridge.jpg)
  ![Ba Na Hills](/images/vietnam/articles/ba-na-hills.jpg)

---

## Day 5: Hoi An
- **Old Town walking tour**
  Discover the lantern-lit streets, historic Chinese assembly halls, and riverside cafés of UNESCO-listed Hoi An.
- **Tailor-made clothing**
  Stop by a local tailor for a quick fitting—Hoi An is famous for fast, quality tailoring.
  ![Hoi An](/images/vietnam/articles/hoi-an.jpg)

---

## Day 6: Hue
- **Imperial City**
  Travel to Hue and visit the Imperial City, where royal palaces, shrines, and the Forbidden Purple City showcase Vietnam’s Nguyen dynasty heritage.
- **Perfume River boat ride**
  Finish the day with a peaceful cruise on the Perfume River.
  ![Hue](/images/vietnam/articles/hue.jpg)

---

## Day 7: Hanoi
- **Old Quarter**
  Arrive in Hanoi and wander the narrow streets of the Old Quarter, where street vendors sell noodle soups and local beer.
- **Hoan Kiem Lake**
  Visit the lake at sunrise or sunset, and see Ngoc Son Temple on the small island.
  ![Hanoi](/images/vietnam/articles/hanoi.jpg)

---

## Day 8: Ha Long Bay
- **Cruise Ha Long Bay**
  Board an overnight cruise through Ha Long Bay’s limestone karsts and emerald waters. Kayak into hidden caves and enjoy fresh seafood onboard.
  ![Ha Long Bay](/images/vietnam/articles/ha-long-bay.jpg)

---

## Day 9-12: Ha Giang Loop
- **Northern mountain road trip**
  Fly or take a night train to Hanoi then travel north toward Ha Giang. Rent a motorcycle or join a guided tour for the loop through dramatic valleys and winding roads.
- **Scenic stops**
  Spend these days exploring Dong Van, Ma Pi Leng Pass, and remote hill-tribe villages. The ever-changing mountain landscape is the highlight of this section.
  ![Ha Giang Loop](/images/vietnam/articles/ha-giang-loop.jpg)

---

## Day 13: Trang An, Ninh Binh and Bai Dinh Pagoda
- **Trang An boat tour**
  Glide through limestone caves and emerald rivers in the scenic Trang An landscape complex.
- **Bai Dinh Pagoda**
  Visit Southeast Asia’s largest pagoda complex and climb to the hilltop statue for wide views over Ninh Binh.
  ![Trang An](/images/vietnam/articles/trang-an.jpg)
  ![Bai Dinh Pagoda](/images/vietnam/articles/bai-dinh-pagoda.jpg)

---

## Day 14: Hanoi and Back
- **Last-minute Hanoi highlights**
  Return to Hanoi for a final stroll through the French Quarter and maybe shop for souvenirs on Hang Gai Street.
- **Departure**
  Head to the airport for your flight home, carrying memories of Vietnam’s cities, coastlines, and mountain roads.
  ![Hanoi Evening](/images/vietnam/articles/hanoi-evening.jpg)

---

**Solo Travel Tips**
- Book domestic flights and train tickets early during peak travel seasons.
- Keep small cash denominations for street food, taxis, and market purchases.
- Stay flexible with the Ha Giang loop itinerary—weather and road conditions can change quickly.
- Try local dishes like pho, bun cha, bánh xèo, and fresh seafood.

This 14-day Vietnam solo itinerary blends cities, culture, landscapes, and adventure from the Mekong Delta to the northern highlands.
