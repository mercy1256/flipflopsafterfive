# Netlify Forms Contact Form Setup Complete! 🎉

Your contact form is now configured to use Netlify Forms!

## 🔧 Required Setup Steps

### 1. No Environment File Needed!
With Netlify Forms, you do not need a `.env.local` file for email configuration or MongoDB for contact form submissions.

## 🚀 What's Now Working

✅ **Contact Form**: Submissions handled by Netlify Forms
✅ **Email Notifications**: You get notified of new submissions (configured via Netlify dashboard)

## 📊 Viewing Submissions

Visit your Netlify dashboard and go to the **Forms** section to view and manage all contact form submissions.

## 🧪 Testing

1. **Deploy your site to Netlify** with the updated code.
2. **Configure Email Notifications** in your Netlify dashboard (Forms -> Notifications).
3. **Test the Contact Form**: Fill out and submit the form at `/contact` on your live site.
4. **Check Your Email**: You should receive a notification from Netlify.

---

**Need help?** Ensure your site is deployed to Netlify and form notifications are configured correctly in your Netlify dashboard!
